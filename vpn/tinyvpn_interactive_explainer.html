01 — KERNEL
Open a TUN device
Create a virtual network interface. The OS routes packets through it — no drivers needed.
⚙
02 — READ
Intercept raw IP packets
Read() from the TUN file descriptor gives you the raw IP packet — Ethernet header stripped.
📦
03 — ENCRYPT
AES-256-GCM seal
Seal the packet with a random nonce. GCM gives you authenticated encryption — tampering detected.
🔐
04 — TUNNEL
Send over UDP
Wrap ciphertext in UDP. The other side decrypts and writes back to its TUN — packet re-emerges.
🌐
